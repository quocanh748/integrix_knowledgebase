This design allows the IX Platform to operate in full synchronization, delivering intelligent, real-time performance across diverse environments. The architecture includes:

## The Architecture of the IX Platform

### Integrated Systems in IX Platform

### **The foundation layer that collects and connects real-time data from the field.**

Integrated Systems form the first layer of the IX Platform, designed to capture and process critical real-time data. Each subsystem can operate independently for its monitoring tasks while also serving as an input data source when connected to the broader platform.

Typical systems include CCTV System, Patrol System, Access Control System (ACS), Remote Alarm Monitoring, Vehicle ACS System, and Fire Alarm System.

By unifying these systems, the IX Platform enables them to act as the “frontline intelligence” — ensuring early incident detection and providing a stable data stream that supports smarter, safer, and more efficient decision-making.

The Core Platform sits at the heart of the IX Platform — the intelligent brain that orchestrates the entire system architecture. As the central processing layer, its Core Modules transform raw input data into real-time intelligence, enabling synchronized decisions and coordinated responses across all integrated systems.

Powered by AI, automation tools, and adaptive workflows, the Core Modules include: DeviceOps Module, AI-Powered Module, Event Handling Module, Admin Center Module, Dashboard Module, and Alert Module.

Serving as the command and decision-making hub, this intelligent Core Platform not only processes and analyzes data but also predicts incidents, prioritizes responses, and maintains an operational ecosystem that is scalable, resilient, and adaptive.

### **The intelligent core that processes, decides, and drives the IX ecosystem.**

## Core Modules in IX Platform

### Control & Response Systems in IX Platform

### **Where artificial intelligence meets real-world action.**

The Control & Response Systems form the final layer of the IX Platform — where artificial intelligence is transformed into real-world actions. This layer delivers alerts, information, and situational awareness to the right people, at the right time, through the right channels.

It includes systems such as the Traffic Signal System, Wall Display System, VMS (Variable Message Sign) Systems, Mobile App Module, Alarm System, and various Standalone Modules — all synchronized with intelligence from the Core Platform to ensure fast, transparent, and effective responses.
