# PSIM – WHEN SECURITY GOES BEYOND MONITORING

From disconnected systems to a unified and controlled security operations environment

8/24/2026

**When systems operate separately**

In many organisations, security and technical systems are deployed separately: video surveillance, access control, fire alarms, intrusion detection, intercom, BMS and other specialised systems.

Each system generates data and alerts. However, when an incident occurs, operators still have to monitor multiple screens, check information from several sources and decide for themselves what to do next.

*The challenge is no longer a lack of equipment, but the ability to connect information and coordinate the response.*

**The role of PSIM**

PSIM – Physical Security Information Management – creates a unified management layer that can:

Integrate multiple security and technical systems into a common operating interface.
Collect, correlate and verify alerts from multiple sources.
Present each situation with the appropriate context, location and priority.
Guide operators through established response procedures.
Record events, decisions and actions for reporting, review and improvement.

**A typical scenario**

When an intrusion alert is triggered, the platform can simultaneously locate the incident on a map, display the relevant camera, check the door status, provide information about the area and guide personnel through each step of the procedure.

The operations centre can therefore do more than see the incident. It can understand the situation, coordinate resources and respond consistently.

**Integration with existing infrastructure**

An important feature of PSIM is its ability to integrate with existing infrastructure. An organisation does not necessarily need to replace all the systems already in use. It can connect them into a unified operating environment and expand the platform in phases.

**From alerts to controlled action**

As the volume of devices and data grows, real value does not come from generating more alerts. It comes from turning those alerts into contextual information and controlled action.

That is the role of PSIM in modern security operations centres.
