# Integration - Intelligence

From Connection to Intelligent Operations

5/30/2026

## Integration is more than connectivity

For many years, Integration has been viewed as the process of connecting technology systems together. CCTV is connected to VMS, access control systems are connected to servers, and dashboards are built to consolidate information.

However, connected systems do not automatically create intelligence.

In reality, many so-called integrated systems operate only at the level of connectivity. Data is exchanged and displayed, but little value is created for operations.

## The challenge is not data

Most organizations today do not suffer from a lack of data.

Their biggest challenge is that data exists across multiple systems, remains disconnected, and is difficult to transform into actionable insights.

Many systems continue to operate as isolated islands, each solving a specific problem but unable to collaborate effectively with others.

## The three foundations of modern integration

To create intelligence, a modern integrated platform must perform three essential functions.

### Unify

Data from multiple systems must be standardized and consolidated into a common language, creating a unified operational view.

### Correlate

Events do not exist independently. A camera alert, an access control event, and a sensor signal may all represent different aspects of the same situation.

Correlating data within a shared context enables the system to understand what is truly happening.

### Orchestrate

Modern platforms should do more than display information. They should support decision-making and trigger appropriate actions to improve operational performance.

## From data to decisions and actions

Without the ability to unify, correlate, and orchestrate:

1,000 cameras remain only 1,000 eyes.
Ten systems remain ten isolated technology islands.
People continue to serve as the central processor for every decision.

The future of Integration is not about connecting more systems. It is about understanding deeper, responding faster, and acting more accurately.
