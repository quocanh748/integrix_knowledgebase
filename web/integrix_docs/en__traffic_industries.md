# **IX Platform for Traffic – Smart Traffic & Integrated Control**

The IX Platform for Traffic connects traffic systems, control centers, and IoT sensors into a unified digital ecosystem. From highways to smart cities, it delivers real-time visibility, predictive analytics, and automated coordination — empowering industries and governments to manage traffic more safely, efficiently, and sustainably.

## **Driving intelligent, connected, and sustainable traffic management.**

## Expressway Control Center

With IX Platform, Integrix Technologies delivers smart traffic solutions to keep expressways safe and efficient:

Integrated Surveillance & Incident Response: Monitor highways in real time with AI-powered CCTV, smart sensors, and centralized video walls for traffic flow and anomaly detection.

Fast Incident Detection & Management: Detect accidents, breakdowns, or wrong-way driving with automated alerts to ensure rapid emergency response and improved road safety.

Smart Lane & Speed Management: Use sensor analytics to dynamically adjust speed limits, lane assignments, and congestion prevention strategies.

## Road Tunnel Control Center

With IX Platform, Integrix Technologies delivers smart traffic solutions to keep tunnels safe and efficient:

Environmental & Safety Monitoring: Monitor tunnel air quality, lighting, and temperature in real time to ensure a safe driving environment under all conditions.

Emergency Detection & Evacuation Support: Detect incidents such as vehicle fires, accidents, or toxic gas leaks, and instantly trigger alarms, ventilation, and emergency exit guidance.

Integrated Traffic & Ventilation Control: Coordinate traffic signals, surveillance cameras, and ventilation systems to maintain airflow and minimize congestion during peak hours or emergencies.

## Urban Road Control Center

IX Platform by Integrix Technologies delivers smart traffic solutions to optimize roadway operations in cities and across regions:

Real-Time Congestion Management: Utilize AI-powered analytics and live camera feeds to monitor traffic flow and dynamically adjust signal timing during peak hours.

Public Transport Priority Integration: Improve urban mobility by giving dynamic signal priority to buses, trams, and emergency vehicles at critical intersections.

Incident Detection & Response Coordination: Quickly detect accidents, breakdowns, or violations and dispatch support teams with real-time situational awareness.

With IX Platform, Integrix Technologies provides smart traffic solutions to optimize signalized intersections and improve mobility:
