**ERP SOFTWARE DEVELOPER INTERN**

**INTRODUCTION**

Integrix Technologies is a technology company specializing in AI-powered integrated platforms, intelligent integration solutions, custom-built software and professional technology services for businesses and infrastructure.

Participate in the research and development of ERP and enterprise software applications.

Participate in Workflow & Process Automation.
Develop Dashboards, Reporting, Authentication & User Management functionalities.

  CRM & Sales
  Purchasing
  Inventory
  Project Management
  Human Resources
  Finance & Accounting Workflows
  Document Management

Third-year, fourth-year students or fresh graduates in:
  Information Technology
  Computer Science
  Software Engineering
  Information Systems
  Computer Engineering
  Or related fields
Basic knowledge of programming, databases and web application development.
Strong willingness to learn and conduct independent research.
Responsible, proactive and able to work effectively in a team.

Personal GitHub account or portfolio.
