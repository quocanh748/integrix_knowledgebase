# AI & SI Internship

Artificial Intelligence & System Integration Internship Program.

6/1/2026

**Introduction**

Integrix Technologies is a technology company specializing in AI-powered integrated platforms, intelligent integration solutions, custom-built software and professional technology services for businesses and infrastructure.

We are looking for students who are passionate about technology and interested in participating in real-world projects related to Artificial Intelligence, software development and system integration.

Assist in researching and developing AI applications.

Support data processing and AI model training activities.

Support the development of AI features across the company's platforms and solutions.
Participate in projects related to:
  AI-Powered Integrated Platforms
  Integrated Security Solutions
  Smart Traffic Solutions
  Intelligent Operations Platforms

**Requirements**

Third-year, fourth-year students or fresh graduates in:
  Artificial Intelligence
  Information Technology
  Computer Science
  Data Science
  Software Engineering
Basic knowledge of Python, Machine Learning or Deep Learning.
Strong willingness to learn and conduct independent research.
Responsible, proactive and self-motivated.
Ability to read and understand English technical documents.

Personal GitHub account or portfolio.
AI-related projects, capstone projects or practical experience.
Familiarity with PyTorch, TensorFlow, OpenCV or LangChain.
Interest in technology systems, software platforms and integrated solutions.
